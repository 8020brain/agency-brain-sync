The app in your menu bar

This is the Agency Brain app. It sits quietly up in your menu bar and keeps the shared folder in sync on your machine, automatically. Leave it running, that's all it needs. No Git, no technical setup, nothing to remember.

Cowork

Claude's desktop workspace, pointed at the shared brain. It already knows your clients, your tone, and how the agency does things.

Skills

Repeatable jobs the team has built and refined: reports, audits, drafts. Run one by name and it does the heavy lifting for you.

Connectors

Google Ads, Sheets and more plug straight in, so Claude works from real, live numbers instead of copy-paste.

Getting going three steps
1
Open Cowork and point it at the brain folder this app keeps in sync on your machine.
2
Ask in plain English, or run a skill by name (for example /client-monthly-review). Browse them all on the Skills tab.
3
Flag anything that falls short on the Flag a skill tab. The Scout improves it for the whole team.
Your Scout has your back

You don't have to work any of this out on your own. Your agency's Scout installs the brain, connects Cowork, and walks you through your first run. Ask them anything, that's what they're there for.

What this dashboard does not track

Deliberately off the table

This view shows how the agency's AI workforce is maturing. It does not watch the people using it.

  • What anyone actually types into Claude. Your team's prompts and conversations are never read, logged, or shown here. What a person asks Claude, pastes in, or talks through stays between them and Claude. This dashboard only ever sees the skills in the shared brain, never how anyone used them.
  • What anyone's skills actually generated
  • Token usage per person
  • Time-of-day activity heatmaps

Tracking the right things signals pride in the team's work. Tracking the wrong things signals distrust.

Flag a skill tell the Scout when a skill fell short

Two ways to flag a skill, whichever's easier. 1. Fill in the quick form. 2. In any Claude session, run /flag-skill — it reads your conversation, drafts an answer to each question, and you just confirm or tweak.

Flag it

One shortcut from inside any session. The skill asks a few short questions, then writes a structured entry the Scout reads next time they open that skill. No bug-report form, no triage queue. If feedback is any harder than this, it never happens.
