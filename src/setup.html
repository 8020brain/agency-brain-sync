Pointing Cowork at your agency brain

  1. Open the Claude desktop app. Click "Open the Claude desktop app" on the wizard, or find Claude in your Applications folder.
  2. Switch to the Cowork tab. Look at the top of the Claude window; the Cowork tab is next to Chat and Code.
  3. Click the folder picker at the bottom of the chat box. It looks like a small folder icon with a name next to it. Click it and choose the folder shown on this wizard screen (the one called agencybrain).
  4. That's it. Claude can now see your agency brain. Any change Claude makes here syncs to your teammates within about a minute.
When connected, the bottom-left will show agencybrain with a folder icon, like this. Click that area in Cowork to attach your folder.
